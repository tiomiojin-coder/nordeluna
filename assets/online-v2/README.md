# Artes Online 0.8

Geradas com a ferramenta integrada ImageGen, sem CLI ou chave de API. Arquivos finais preservados neste projeto e copiados para `game-plugins/Nordeluna Online/assets/`:

- `luna-coin.png`: RGBA, 1254×1254, fundo transparente verificado. O runtime cria uma textura de 32×32 uma vez e descarta a imagem original da memória.
- `menu-background.png`: RGB, 1448×1086. O runtime cria uma textura de 512×384 uma vez e a reutiliza nos painéis.

Os PNGs originais não foram redesenhados por scripts. O texto, as seleções e os botões são desenhados pelo jogo e continuam navegáveis pelo direcional. Prévias de layout usam uma fonte aproximada e o desenho dos métodos reais da interface.

## Prompt final — Luna Coin

Create one production game inventory icon for the exclusive fictional currency 'Luna Coin' in Pokémon Nordeluna. One single circular golden coin viewed straight-on, thick dark navy pixel outline, elegant teal crescent moon embossed in the center with one tiny four-point star, warm gold rim and ivory highlights. Beautiful polished 16-bit JRPG pixel art with a deliberately simple silhouette readable when reduced to 24 or 32 pixels. Center the coin occupying 78 percent of a square canvas. Genuinely transparent background outside the coin, no ground shadow, no glow outside the silhouette. No text, no letters, no numerals, no watermark, no scene, no extra coins, no spritesheet. This is a standalone usable transparent game asset, not a mockup.

## Prompt final — fundo do menu

Create a production background texture for a handheld 16-bit JRPG online menu for the fictional moon-themed game Pokémon Nordeluna. Landscape 4:3 composition. Extremely dark midnight navy and indigo, tasteful deep teal celestial observatory atmosphere. A small elegant golden crescent and tiny constellation in the upper-right corner; restrained fine gold and turquoise ornamental pixel accents only at the extreme outer edges. The central 85 percent and the whole left side must remain nearly uniform very dark navy with low detail, ample negative space for readable menu options that will be drawn separately by the game. Refined and beautiful nostalgic pixel art, crisp controlled palette, understated, not neon, not cluttered. No text, no letters, no UI buttons, no icons, no interface mockup, no characters, no logos, no watermark. Deliver only the usable rectangular background artwork.
